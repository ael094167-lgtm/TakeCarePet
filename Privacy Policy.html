Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 11, 2026

PawCare AI respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have when using the PawCare AI application and related services.

By using PawCare AI, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Account credentials
  • User ID
  • Account creation date
  • Subscription status

We use this information to create and manage your PawCare account.

Pet Information

You may provide information about your pets, including:

  • Pet name
  • Species
  • Breed
  • Age or date of birth
  • Gender
  • Weight
  • Medical and health-related information
  • Vaccination records
  • Medication information
  • Care routines
  • Reminders
  • Other information you voluntarily provide about your pet

This information is used to personalize PawCare's features and recommendations.

Photos

If you use PawCare's AI Photo Scan feature, you may upload photos of your pet.

Photos are processed to provide AI-powered educational insights about visible characteristics or potential concerns.

You should avoid uploading photographs containing unnecessary personal information, such as your face, home address, identification documents, or other sensitive information.

Chat and AI Interactions

When you use PawCare AI Chat or other AI-powered features, we may collect:

  • Your messages
  • Information you provide about your pet
  • AI-generated responses
  • Scan-related information
  • Interaction history necessary to provide the service

This information allows PawCare to provide personalized responses and maintain your experience.

Usage Information

We may automatically collect limited technical and usage information, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • General usage activity
  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Authentication and session information

We use this information to maintain, secure, and improve PawCare.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide PawCare's features and services
  • Store and manage pet profiles
  • Provide AI-powered chat and photo analysis
  • Personalize care recommendations
  • Manage reminders and routines
  • Track health and care activities
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Improve app performance and reliability

  • Maintain security

3. AI-Powered Features

PawCare uses artificial intelligence to provide educational pet-care information and personalized assistance.

AI-generated information may be based on information you provide, including pet profiles, messages, and uploaded photos.

AI responses are provided for general educational and informational purposes only. PawCare AI is not a veterinarian and does not provide veterinary diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical services.

For serious, urgent, or potentially life-threatening situations, contact a qualified veterinarian or emergency veterinary service.
