TakeCarePet Support

We're here to help.

TakeCarePet helps pet parents better understand and manage their pets' health with AI-powered tools, health tracking, reminders, and personalized insights. If you need help with the app, we're here for you.

Account & Login

Having trouble creating an account, signing in, or accessing your pet profile? Make sure you're using the email address associated with your TakeCarePet account. If the problem continues, contact our support team.

AI Chat

TakeCarePet's AI Chat provides general pet-health information and guidance.

AI responses are informational and should not replace professional veterinary advice. For emergencies or serious symptoms, contact a qualified veterinarian immediately.

AI Photo Scan

You can use the AI Photo Scan to get general information about visible signs in your pet's photo. For the most accurate results, upload a clear, well-lit photo showing the relevant area.

Pet Profile

You can manage your pet's information directly from the app, including their name, species, breed, and other health-related information.

Subscriptions

If you have questions about your subscription or in-app purchases, please check your Apple App Store subscription settings.

Privacy & Security

Important Health Disclaimer

TakeCarePet provides AI-powered information for educational and informational purposes only.

TakeCarePet does not provide veterinary diagnoses, treatment, or emergency medical services. AI-generated information may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Always consult a qualified veterinarian for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

If your pet is experiencing a medical emergency, contact a veterinarian or emergency animal hospital immediately.
